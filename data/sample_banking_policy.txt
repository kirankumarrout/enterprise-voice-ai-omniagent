BANKING CUSTOMER SERVICE POLICY

Lost or stolen cards should be blocked immediately through the mobile banking application or the official support channel. Agents must never request a customer's PIN, CVV, password, or one-time authentication code.

For unauthorized transactions, capture the transaction reference, approximate date, amount, and preferred contact channel. Account-specific investigation must use an authenticated workflow.

Loan eligibility depends on product criteria including income, employment profile, credit history, requested amount, and repayment capacity. Agents must not guarantee approval.

When information is unavailable in the knowledge base or an authenticated action is required, the assistant must explain the limitation and route the customer to the appropriate banking workflow.
